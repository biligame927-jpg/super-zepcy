About the Collection

Super Zipcy is a public sale open to every wallet: 8888 works, all unrevealed at sale, shaped by the aesthetic of Korean illustrator Zipcy. After minting, you hold a sealed token. The reveal date has not been announced. There is no whitelist and no free mint. The public sale price is fixed at 0.6 ETH, with a maximum of 2 per wallet.

Zipcy is a Korean illustrator known for intimate portraits and softly lit figures. Super Zipcy brings that visual language on-chain as 8888 unique 1/1 works. Images on this page are style references only; the final artwork for each token will appear after the official reveal, whose date has not been announced.

How to Mint

How to Mint

  1. 01

    Connect your wallet

    Use the button in the header or on the mint card to connect your Ethereum wallet, and confirm you are on Ethereum Mainnet.

  2. 02

    Choose a quantity

    Mint 1 or 2 in a single transaction. A wallet may mint no more than 2 in total.

  3. 03

    Pay the exact amount

    The contract accepts only the exact public-sale price: 0.6 ETH multiplied by the quantity you choose. If the amount sent does not match, the transaction will fail and nothing will be minted. Confirm you are on Ethereum Mainnet and that your wallet has enough ETH for the price plus gas before you confirm.

Questions

FAQ

Can I mint now?

No. Minting Not Started. You can connect your wallet on this site, but minting has not opened yet.

What are the price, limits, and royalty?

The public sale price is fixed at 0.6 ETH. Each wallet may mint a maximum of 2, and no more than 2 in a single transaction. Secondary-market royalty is 5%.

Will I see the artwork immediately after minting?

No. All 8888 works remain unrevealed during the public sale. You will hold a sealed token. The reveal date has not been announced.

When do the airdrop and trading begin?

The airdrop will be sent on December 25, with trading to follow.

What benefits do holders receive?

Announced benefits include a private community, airdrop rewards, priority whitelist access, merch and art prints, and private events. The airdrop is scheduled for December 25, with trading to follow. Further details will be shared later.
